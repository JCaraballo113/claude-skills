# Delight

How to give a bridge personality without reopening the gulf it closes: delight is **feedback with personality** — it layers onto a bridged flow, never substitutes for the bridge. A confetti burst over an unbridged gulf is decoration on a hazard.

Vocabulary from [LANGUAGE.md](LANGUAGE.md). Apply during phase 3, once the bridge's shape is settled and its states are being designed: `impeccable` owns the visual bar, this file owns *when feedback earns personality and how much*.

## The five moments

Personality pays only at these feedback moments; everywhere else, plain feedback is the default.

**Micro-action acknowledgment** (a like, a save, a toggle)
Instant and warm — a state change with a brief animation, budget **100–200ms**. Longer, and the acknowledgment becomes friction on every repetition of the action.

**Gesture affordances** (swipe to archive, drag to dismiss)
A gesture is an **affordance** with a weak **signifier** by construction. If a bridge uses one: signify it during the motion (color + icon revealed as the user drags) and keep undo within reach — a fast path with no recovery converts an execution shortcut into an evaluation gulf.

**Celebration on Job completion**
Celebrate the **Job** completing — never intermediate steps, or the celebration becomes noise and the real milestone has nothing left to mark it. Non-blocking: the user never waits for applause.

**The designed wait** (loading states)
A designed wait shrinks *perceived* time even when actual time is fixed — legibility first (what's happening, how much is left), personality second. Entertainment *instead of* a progress signal is an evaluation gulf with a mascot.

**The empathetic error**
The bridge itself is [BRIDGING.md](BRIDGING.md)'s next-action tactic; delight here is purely *tone* — plain human language, no blame, no raw codes as the message. Personality only after the recovery path is obvious.

## Restraint

Delight should delight, not overwhelm. Beyond the per-moment budgets above:

- **Firsts and milestones.** Celebrate first-time completions and countable milestones (10th, streak kept, personal best). Routine repetitions get plain completion feedback.
- **One moment per Job.** When several qualify at once, collapse them into the single biggest milestone; the rest render plain. Overwhelm is stacking.
- **High stakes mute play.** Payments, destructive actions, errors involving loss, security: personality yields to calm, plain clarity. Warmth stays; play goes — a joke over lost work reads as mockery.

`prefers-reduced-motion` is a floor, not a preference: every animated moment ships a motionless variant (color or opacity change).
